## Project relevance

This repository is a fork of the upstream Ollama project. Upstream maintains its runtime, model distribution and platform installers. Local portfolio work should focus on reproducible model/runtime experiments, platform compatibility and focused upstream fixes while preserving upstream README, contributor guidance, licence and AGENTS.md.
